# Privacy Policy for Taliq

**Effective Date:** [Date]

**Last Updated:** [Date]

MiddleMind ("we," "us," or "our") operates Taliq, an AI-powered HR management platform designed for Saudi Arabian businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal data in compliance with the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) (Royal Decree M/19).

This policy is available in Arabic and English. In case of any discrepancy, the Arabic version prevails.

## 1. Definitions
- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including names, identification numbers, contact details, biometric data, and employment records.
- **Sensitive Personal Data:** Includes health data, biometric data, genetic data, religious beliefs, and data revealing racial or ethnic origin.
- **Controller:** MiddleMind, responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing personal data.
- **Processor:** Any third party engaged by us to process data on our behalf.
- **Data Subject:** Individuals whose personal data we process (e.g., employees, candidates, administrators).

## 2. Data We Collect
We collect personal data necessary for providing HR services, including:

### Employee Data:
- Basic information: Name, ID number, contact details, nationality, date of birth.
- Employment details: Position, salary, benefits, performance records.
- Sensitive data: Health information for benefits, biometric data for attendance (if applicable).

### Candidate Data:
- Application details: Resume, interview notes, background checks.
- Contact information.

### Administrator Data:
- Account credentials, usage logs.

Data is collected directly from you, your employer, or authorized sources.

## 3. Purposes of Processing
We process personal data for the following lawful purposes:
- Managing employment relationships and payroll.
- Recruitment and onboarding.
- Compliance with Saudi Labor Law and PDPL.
- Improving our services through analytics.
- Security and fraud prevention.

Processing is limited to what is necessary for these purposes.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing
Processing is based on:
- **Consent:** Explicit consent for sensitive data or marketing.
- **Contractual Necessity:** For employment contracts.
- **Legitimate Interests:** For business operations, provided they do not override your rights.
- **Legal Obligations:** Compliance with laws.

## 5. Data Subject Rights
Under PDPL, you have the following rights:
- **Access:** Request information about your data.
- **Correction:** Rectify inaccurate data.
- **Deletion:** Request erasure where lawful.
- **Objection:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Withdrawal of Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at [email/phone].

We will respond within 30 days, extendable by 30 days if necessary.

## 6. Data Sharing and Transfers
We do not sell personal data. Sharing occurs only:
- With your employer for HR purposes.
- With service providers (e.g., cloud hosting) under contracts ensuring PDPL compliance.
- For legal compliance.

International transfers require SDAIA approval or adequate safeguards.

## 7. Data Security
We implement technical and organizational measures to protect data:
- Encryption, access controls, regular audits.
- Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk processing.
- Breach notification to SDAIA within 72 hours and affected individuals promptly.

## 8. Data Retention
Data is retained only as long as necessary:
- Employment data: Duration of employment plus 5 years (per Saudi Labor Law).
- Candidate data: 1 year after application.
- Deletion upon request or legal expiry.

## 9. Cookies and Tracking
Our website uses cookies for functionality. You can manage preferences via browser settings.

## 10. Children's Data
We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 without parental consent.
